> **Developed as part of the DiGiLIANS (Digital Pioneers Initiative) Data Analytics Track**

An interactive **Power BI** HR Analytics dashboard that transforms raw human resources data into actionable workforce insights, enabling HR teams and decision-makers to monitor employee trends, understand attrition drivers, and make data-driven retention decisions.

![HR Analytics Dashboard Preview](images/dashboard.png)

---

## 🎯 Key KPIs Tracked
* **Total Employees** & **Active Employees**
* **Employees Left** & **Overall Attrition Rate**
* **Overtime Employees**
* **Average Monthly Salary**

---

## 📊 Business Insights & Analysis
* **Attrition Breakdown:** Analyzed employee turnover across departments and job roles to pinpoint high-risk areas.
* **Overtime vs. Turnover:** Identified the relationship between employee overtime and attrition rates.
* **Workforce Demographics:** Explored headcount distribution by department and gender.
* **Compensation Analysis:** Compared average monthly salaries across different departments.
* **Interactive Exploration:** Enabled dynamic filtering and cross-visual slicing to monitor workforce trends effortlessly.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills
* **BI & Visualization:** Microsoft Power BI
* **ETL & Data Prep:** Power Query, Data Cleaning
* **Modeling & Calculations:** Data Modeling, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

---

## 🚀 How to View
1. Download the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2. Open it using **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the interactive slicers and filters to explore attrition, salary, and workforce trends across departments and roles.
